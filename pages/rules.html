The Basics

Player Position & Sides

[cite_start]

Padel is played in pairs [cite: 1][cite_start], with players positioned on either side of the net[cite: 2]. [cite_start]The serving player initiates the point, and the receiving player returns the ball[cite: 3]. [cite_start]While the receiver must be in their area, their partner and the server's partner can stand anywhere on their respective sides of the court[cite: 4]. [cite_start]Players are required to switch sides of the court whenever the total number of games played in a set is an odd number[cite: 5]. [cite_start]There is a 90-second maximum rest period between games[cite: 7].

Scoring

[cite_start]

Scoring in Padel is identical to tennis[cite: 62].

    [cite_start]
  • Points are scored as 15, 30, and 40. The fourth point wins the game[cite: 63].
  • If both pairs reach 40, the score is called "deuce." [cite_start]The next point won is "advantage"[cite: 64, 65]. [cite_start]The same pair must win the next point to secure the game; otherwise, the score returns to deuce[cite: 66].
    • [cite_start]
  • A set is won by the first pair to win 6 games with at least a two-game lead[cite: 68].
    • [cite_start]
  • If the score reaches 6-6, a "tie-break" is played to decide the set[cite: 68, 71]. [cite_start]The first pair to 7 points, with a two-point margin, wins the tie-break and the set 7-6[cite: 72, 75].

The Serve

Serving Motion

    [cite_start]
  • The server must have both feet behind the service line when serving[cite: 11].
    • [cite_start]
  • Before hitting the ball, the server must bounce it on the ground behind the service line[cite: 14].
    • [cite_start]
  • The ball must be struck at or below waist level[cite: 17]. [cite_start]At least one foot must be on the ground at the moment of impact[cite: 17].
    • [cite_start]
  • Walking, running, or jumping while serving is not permitted, though small foot movements that don't alter the initial stance are allowed[cite: 18].
    • [cite_start]
  • If the server swings and misses the ball, the serve is counted[cite: 19].

Service Target & Faults

    [cite_start]
  • The serve is hit diagonally over the net into the opponent's service box[cite: 12].
    • [cite_start]
  • A serve is a fault if it bounces outside the opponent's service box (the lines are in)[cite: 24].
    • [cite_start]
  • A serve is also a fault if the ball hits the server's partner [cite: 25][cite_start], or if it hits the server's own wall before crossing the net[cite: 27].
    • [cite_start]
  • After bouncing in the correct service box, if the ball hits the wire fence before a second bounce, it is a fault[cite: 26]. Hitting a glass wall is permitted.
    • [cite_start]
  • The server has a second serve if the first one is a fault[cite: 47].

Gameplay & Points

Returning the Ball

    [cite_start]
  • The receiver must let the ball bounce once in their service area before returning the serve[cite: 38].
    • [cite_start]
  • During a rally, a player can hit a volley (hitting the ball before it bounces), except when returning a serve[cite: 90].
    • [cite_start]
  • Players are allowed to hit the ball after it has bounced on their court and then rebounded off one of their walls[cite: 88, 98].
    • [cite_start]
  • A return is correct if the ball hits the net or posts and then lands in the opponent's court[cite: 93].

Losing a Point

A pair loses a point if:

    [cite_start]
  • The ball bounces twice on their side of the court before being returned[cite: 105].
    • [cite_start]
  • A player, their racquet, or their clothing touches the net or the opponent's court while the ball is in play[cite: 101].
    • [cite_start]
  • A player volleys the ball before it has crossed over the net to their side[cite: 106].
    • [cite_start]
  • The ball hits a player or anything they are wearing, other than their racquet[cite: 108].
    • [cite_start]
  • A player hits the ball twice in a row [cite: 107][cite_start], or both players on a team touch the ball either simultaneously or consecutively[cite: 111].

Let (Point Repetition)

A point is replayed as a "let" if:

    [cite_start]
  • On a serve, the ball touches the net and then lands in the correct service box[cite: 53]. [cite_start]If this happens on a second serve, the server only gets one more attempt[cite: 56].
    • [cite_start]
  • The ball breaks or splits during a point[cite: 57].
    • [cite_start]
  • Play is interrupted by an unforeseen event outside of the players' control[cite: 58].

Conduct & Procedures

Continuous Play & Delays

    [cite_start]
  • Play must be continuous throughout the match [cite: 114][cite_start], except for permitted rest periods[cite: 115].
    • [cite_start]
  • Players are allowed 90 seconds to change sides [cite: 143] [cite_start]and a maximum of 25 seconds between points[cite: 143]. [cite_start]Unjustified delays can result in a warning from the umpire[cite: 144].
    • [cite_start]
  • If a player is injured, they are allowed 3 minutes for medical attention[cite: 145]. [cite_start]Loss of physical condition due to muscle ache or fatigue is not considered a valid reason for delay[cite: 147].

Clothing & Equipment

  • Players must wear clean and appropriate clothing. [cite_start]Sleeveless tops and swimsuits are not allowed[cite: 135].
    • [cite_start]
  • In team matches, wearing the same clothes is recommended but not mandatory[cite: 137].

Behavior & Discipline

    [cite_start]
  • Players must behave in a courteous and polite manner at all times[cite: 140].
    • [cite_start]
  • Players cannot leave the play area during a match without the umpire's permission, except to change sides[cite: 141].
    • [cite_start]
  • Audible and visible obscenities are prohibited[cite: 148, 149].
    • [cite_start]
  • Violently hitting the ball out of play or throwing the racquet are considered abuse and will be penalized[cite: 150].
    • [cite_start]
  • Aggressive or unsportsmanlike behavior towards officials, opponents, or spectators will be penalized by the head umpire[cite: 151].
